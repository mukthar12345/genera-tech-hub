Our Story

Genera Tech Hub was founded with a clear purpose — to bring honest, reliable, and professional technology services to Nigerian customers.

We saw that many people were struggling to find trustworthy places for phone and laptop repairs, quality devices, and fair trade-in offers. We decided to change that.

Today, we serve customers with:

  • Quality phone and laptop sales
  • Expert repair services
  • Software installation and upgrades
  • SSD and RAM upgrades
  • Trade-in and swap options
  • Honest, transparent pricing
